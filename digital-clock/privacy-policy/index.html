Privacy Policy: Digital Clock

Last updated: 2026-11-10

Digital Clock ("the app", package com.skojio.digitalclock) is a full-screen clock for Android, published by Skojio. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What stays on your device

  • The app has no account, no sign-in and no cloud sync.
  • Your settings (colors, font, time format, brightness, auto-dim and similar choices) are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address, and
  • diagnostics and usage data about the ad and the app.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Pro users see no ads, and the app does not request ads for them.

Consent choices (EU and similar regions)

Where required by law, the app shows Google's consent form on first launch. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy choices. That option appears only in regions where the consent form applies.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. We receive only confirmation from Google Play that Pro has been purchased, which the app uses to unlock Pro features and remembers on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
